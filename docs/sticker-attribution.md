# RVU Kids picture stickers
86 stickers are adapted from Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D artwork, licensed under the MIT license:
https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji/blob/main/LICENSE
https://github.com/shuding/fluentui-emoji-unicode
Microsoft Corporation copyright notices and MIT conditions apply.
The remaining 15 images are new RVU Kids pictograms generated by scripts/build-word-stickers.py.
Each picture is a 192×192 transparent WebP illustration with its own vocabulary word mapping.
These are standalone subject illustrations, not excerpts of the original book's printed pages.
