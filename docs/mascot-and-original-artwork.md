- The chameleon is the exact user-provided transparent mascot, optimized to `assets/mascot/chameleon.webp`. Do **not** crop the original book's printed chameleon.
- The first 25 high-traffic illustrated words (A–G including Fish) were newly illustrated and split into individual transparent WebP assets.
- The remaining 76 original words still use individually mapped transparent WebP artwork. They are not crops of book pages but require artwork replacement to achieve the same exact book-like painted style.
- Young boy/girl selectors use new independent vector avatars in `assets/avatars/` rather than clipping children's heads from printed pages.
- Separate letter names and phonemes remain available in education; the homepage introduction plays only Apple and its sentence. The letter A is printed without a letter audio button.
- Audio source is an American English neural voice pair; human pronunciation review remains pending, despite automated 488-file decoding and clipping checks.
